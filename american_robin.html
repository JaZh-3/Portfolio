American Robin (Turdus migratorius)

Information

Scientific name

Turdus migratorius

Family

Turdidae

Order

Passeriformes

Conservation Status

Least Concern

Overview

The american robin (Turdus migratorius) is a medium-sized bird part of the thrush family. Commonly seen in backyards or trees, it is easily recognizable for its orange breast and cheerful song.

Distribution

Source: AllAboutBirds

Legend

  • Blue - year-round
  • Red - breeding
  • Yellow - migration
  • Light blue - Non-breeding

Due to its abundant and largely distrubuted area, the American robin can be found all over Ontario. However, it is usually seen year-round in the southern-most part of the province.
