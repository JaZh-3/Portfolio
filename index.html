Common Birds..

Northern Cardinal (Cardinalis cardinalis)
The northern cardinal (Cardinalis cardinalis) is a medium-sized bird that is recognizable for its vibrant red colors and its songs.
American Robin (Turdus migratorius)
A spring icon, the American robin (Turdus migratorius) is a medium-sized bird that features a familiar tune and orange belly.
Canada Goose(Branta canadensis)
Usually seen in flocks, Canada goose (Branta canadensis) is a large bird that is quite aggressive and common to spot in parks.
Mourning dove (Zenaida macroura)
The mourning dove (Zenaida macroura), a member of the pigeon family, is well known for its familiar song, consisting of coos.
